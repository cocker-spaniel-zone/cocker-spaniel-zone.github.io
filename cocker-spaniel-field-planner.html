02 / How to run it

Three jobs, same order

  1. Nose first

    Sniffing lowers arousal. Do the nose job before the walk, not after.

  2. Body second

    Retrieve, water or training: one job that actually tires the dog.

  3. Off-switch last

    Chew, settle or place work. The week only works if the calm job happens.

  4. Tick and repeat

    Print the card, tick what happened and repeat the jobs that worked.

03 / Questions

Before you start

Is this a training programme?

No. It is an enrichment planner. It gives a Cocker Spaniel a predictable week of sniffing, movement and settling. For behaviour problems, work with a qualified trainer or behaviourist.

Why three jobs a day?

Because most owners already walk the dog. The missing pieces are usually nose work and an off-switch, so the plan adds those without lengthening the day much.

Can I use it with a puppy?

Yes, but the puppy version shortens each job and adds more sleep. Keep sessions short, stop before the dog loses focus and skip anything that looks like forced exercise.

Does it work without a garden?

Yes. Flat and house plans use hallway searches, scatter feeds, towel games and indoor retrieves. A garden only changes where the search happens.
